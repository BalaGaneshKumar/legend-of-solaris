# The Legend of Solaris

A fictional illustrated story across four series (eight books) about Bala's Solaris, shown as page-turning books on a web page.
It works on phones, tablets and computers.

- Open the site: `https://balaganeshkumar.github.io/legend-of-solaris/`
- Open one book directly, for example: `.../#book-p41`
  (ids: `vamp`, `solaris`, `p311`, `p312`, `p321`, `p322`, `p41`, `p42`)

The site is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. It has no server, no login,
no forms, no cookies and no tracking.

## Series structure

| Series | Books | On the site |
|---|---|---|
| Series 1 | 1 | The Legend of Vamp |
| Series 2 | 1 | The Legend of Bala's Solaris |
| Series 3 | 4 | 3.11 The Bored Sorcerer, 3.12 The Rescue, 3.21 The Wanderer, 3.22 The Queen of Solaris |
| Series 4 | 2 | 4.1 The Four Lights, 4.2 The Office |

## Status

- Series 2 reflects Bala's Solaris app as it exists today.
- Series 1, 3 and 4 are story only.
- Vamp and her four companions are not built yet; their features haven't been started.

## Fictional and hypothetical

The story is an imagined version of the app and its possible future.
The characters are not real people.

## Why this exists

This project is a way to learn beyond code with AI. AI makes it possible to build things I couldn't build alone, but I stay careful with anything I don't fully understand.
